PURCHASE TERMS SUMMARY

NAVRYNT Business Source

This page summarizes the published commercial scope for the standard NAVRYNT 1.0 Business Source offer. The delivered LICENSE, verified payment/order record, and any separately signed agreement control the actual grant.

LICENSED ENTITY

One legal entity

The standard grant covers one licensed legal entity.

USERS

25 internal authorized users

Employees and contractors acting solely for the licensed entity count toward the limit and should be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

PRODUCTION

3 production deployments

Reasonable development, CI, staging, backup and short-lived test environments for the same entity are permitted separately.

SOURCE

Internal modifications

Full first-party source code is included and may be modified for permitted internal use.

VERSION

Perpetual Navrynt 1.x

Lawfully received Navrynt 1.x releases may be used perpetually. Future 2.x rights are not included unless expressly stated in writing.

RESTRICTIONS

No redistribution / SaaS / OEM

Redistribution, resale, sublicensing, public source release, third-party SaaS/managed-service, OEM and service-provider rights are not included by default.

Direct purchase workflow

The public website can generate a buyer reference locally in the browser and prepare purchase emails. That browser-generated reference is not proof of payment and does not activate a license. Delivery occurs only after the seller verifies payment.

BANK TRANSFER

Manual verification

Use the exact NAVRYNT purchase reference in the transfer description and email the transfer receipt for verification.

EMAIL / PROCUREMENT

Direct seller contact

Invoice, PO, vendor-onboarding, and purchase requests can be prepared locally and sent using the buyer's own email application.

EXTERNAL MARKETPLACE

Optional and separate

A separate marketplace link may be offered for buyers who prefer card payment. The NAVRYNT website and product runtime do not depend on that marketplace.

Native product license

Each paid Business Source grant can be represented by a unique seller-signed NVY1 token / .nvylic file verified locally with Ed25519. Runtime validation is independent of the payment channel.

Third-party libraries, model runtimes, APIs, datasets, checkpoints and other assets retain their own licenses. Customers are responsible for lawful data collection/use, model and dataset rights, consent or other lawful basis, security, retention, access control and compliance obligations.

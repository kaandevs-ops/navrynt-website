WEBSITE PRIVACY

Static by design.

The NAVRYNT official website is designed to run entirely on GitHub Pages without a NAVRYNT commerce backend, database, tracking API, purchase webhook, or server-side customer account system.

PURCHASE FORMS

Processed in your browser

Buyer information entered on the purchase page is used locally by JavaScript to prepare a reference, downloadable summary, print view, or email message. The website itself does not submit that form to a NAVRYNT server.

EMAIL

You choose when to send

When you choose an email purchase or payment-confirmation action, the website opens your email application with a prepared message. The email is sent only after you send it from your own mail client.

EXTERNAL LINKS

Separate services have separate policies

If you leave this website for the public demo, GitHub, or an optional external marketplace, that destination's own privacy and service terms apply.

SECRETS

No seller credentials in public source

Seller signing keys, customer licenses, bank login credentials, marketplace secrets, and customer source packages are not embedded in the public website repository.

HOSTING

GitHub Pages

Normal hosting-level request logs or telemetry may be handled by GitHub under its own policies. NAVRYNT does not add a custom analytics backend in this package.

LAUNCH NOTE

Finalize legal identity details

Before public commercial launch, the seller should publish any legal-entity, jurisdiction, retention, tax, and contact disclosures required by applicable law.
